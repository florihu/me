About

I am a PhD student at the Vienna University of Economics and Business, working on the political economy and ecology of copper metabolism. The project focuses on building a large-scale Physical Supply Use Table (PSUT) to track copper through its life cycle, from extraction to manufacturing to end of life and vice versa. To (1) reconcile different data sources and (2) disaggregate flexibly along dimensions, I am working on a generalizable open Python-based library for Supply-Use Table Optimisation (pySUTO). Further, I want to contextualise the physical side of the copper metabolism with critical political economy approaches to understand and explain how social structure configures physical structure
